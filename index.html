The Story Behind Sheger Barber

About Story Placeholder

Founded by Eyob Tesfaye, Sheger Barber was created to bring back the timeless art of neighborhood barbershop hospitality to Addis Ababa. Located right inside the iconic Agona Cinema Compound, our vision is simple: provide every customer with top-tier haircuts, clean razor line-ups, and a welcoming environment where you feel right at home.

Whether you need a quick refresh before a meeting or a relaxing hot wash and beard trim on the weekend, Eyob ensures personalized care for every gentleman who sits in our chairs.
